Good to know

Questions before you book

What does the $125 cover?
A standard cleaning of one accessible residential drain through an existing cleanout. If your setup is more involved, we’ll tell you what it takes and quote it before we touch anything.
What if snaking isn’t enough?
A snake bores a hole through a clog; grease, scale and roots stay coated on the pipe wall and rebuild. If we find that’s the real issue, we’ll show you and quote hydro jetting — a full-diameter clean — before proceeding. Your call.
Do you offer emergency drain service?
Yes. We dispatch 24/7 and run daily routes through Lee, Charlotte, Collier, Hendry and Sarasota. Active backups get priority — call rather than fill in the form for the fastest truck.
