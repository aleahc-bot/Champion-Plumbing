Good to know

Questions before you book

How often is backflow testing required?
Annually in most jurisdictions here. We handle the certified test, any repair the assembly needs, and filing the report with the authority — plus a reminder before your next due date so you never miss it.
What happens if my assembly fails?
We can usually repair or rebuild it on the same visit. We’ll show you what failed and quote the repair upfront before doing the work, then re-test and file the passing report.
Do you handle commercial and multi-site accounts?
Yes. Restaurants, plazas, HOAs and property managers get one point of contact and a managed calendar, so every assembly across every location stays certified and on file.
