Our story

Started with a truck
and a reputation.

Sam is a third-generation plumber, born and raised in Florida. He enlisted in the United States Marine Corps, served honourably, and came back to work for his father's plumbing company — ten years of learning the trade properly before striking out on his own.

He moved to Cape Coral to carry that trade forward and build a full-service company for his new neighbours. Champion Plumbing Sewer & Drain grew out of that: drain cleaning and hydro jetting, camera inspections, certified backflow testing and rebuilds, water heaters, re-pipes, and maintenance contracts for restaurants and homes across the region.

What hasn't changed is who picks up the phone. Call after hours and you'll reach someone who can actually dispatch a truck — not a call centre that takes a message and hopes for the best.

The Marine Corps part shows up in how the work gets done: show up when you said you would, do it right the first time, and leave the place better than you found it. That's not marketing language. It's just the standard.
