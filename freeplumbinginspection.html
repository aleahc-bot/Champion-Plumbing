Good to know

Questions before you book

Is it really free — any catch?
It’s free, and there’s no obligation to book work. If we find something, we’ll explain it and quote it upfront. If everything looks good, we’ll tell you that too — we’d rather earn a customer than push a job.
What’s included in the inspection?
A visual walk-through of your accessible plumbing: fixtures, supply lines, drains, water heater, and shut-off valves, plus a look for leaks and corrosion. You get a clear summary of anything worth watching.
Do you inspect septic systems too?
We can take a look and flag obvious concerns, but a full septic inspection with pumping is a separate service. We’ll let you know if that’s the smarter next step.
