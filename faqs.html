Septic systems

How often should a septic tank be pumped?
For most Southwest Florida households, every three to five years. The real answer depends on tank size and how many people are in the house — a family of five on a 900-gallon tank needs it far more often than a couple on a 1,500. If you've just bought a property and nobody can tell you the last service date, get it pumped and start the clock fresh.
What are the warning signs of a failing septic system?
Slow drains throughout the house rather than in one fixture, gurgling from toilets, standing water or unusually green grass over the drain field, and smell in the yard. Any of those means call before it becomes a backup inside the house.
Do you service ATU aerobic systems?
Yes — maintenance, component replacement and new installs. Aerobic treatment units need more regular attention than a conventional tank because of the aerator, and a lot of companies in the area won't take them on. We will.

Drains & hydro jetting

What's the difference between snaking and hydro jetting?
A snake bores a hole through the middle of a clog. Water drains again, but everything coated on the pipe wall is still there, so the blockage rebuilds. Jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the full inner diameter — grease, scale and roots come out with it. Snaking is a quick fix; jetting is a clean pipe.
Is hydro jetting safe for older pipes?
In most cases, yes — pressure is adjustable and we match it to the line. That's exactly why we camera the pipe first. If we find a cracked or badly deteriorated section, we'll tell you before jetting rather than after.
Why does my drain keep backing up after it's cleared?
Usually because the underlying cause was never found. Repeat backups often point to something structural — a belly holding water, root intrusion at a joint, or a full septic tank. Clearing the blockage without a camera run just resets the timer.

Commercial & compliance

How often do grease traps need cleaning?
It depends on volume and trap size, but most kitchens land somewhere between monthly and quarterly. We'll assess your setup and set a schedule that keeps you compliant without paying for service you don't need — and we manage the calendar so you don't have to.
Do you provide compliance manifests?
Yes. Every grease trap service comes with documentation for your records and your health inspector.
Can you work outside business hours?
That's how most of our commercial work runs. Early mornings, late nights, whatever keeps the kitchen open and the dining room clear.
How often is backflow testing required?
Annually in most jurisdictions here. We handle the certified test, any repair the assembly needs, and filing the report with the authority — plus a reminder before your next due date.
